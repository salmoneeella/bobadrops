Hack4Philly

What is Hack4Philly?

Hack4Philly is a city-wide initiative collaborating with some of Philadelphia's top high schools, including Central, Masterman, Carver Engineering and Science, and Northeast High School, to turn high schoolers into startup founders. Through hands-on workshops and project based learning, we teach students how to build their ideas, equipping them with technical skills such as coding, UI/UX design, and product management. From web development, to using Figma, along with pitching their projects, students turn concepts into creations. Hack4Philly is also partnered with Hack Club to reward members for what they make.
